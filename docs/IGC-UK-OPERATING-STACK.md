## 1. NICHE CONFIRMATION

**Target:** Independent UK IT MSPs (Managed Service Providers), 5–50 employees, owner-operated, selling managed IT services / helpdesk / cybersecurity / Microsoft-and-cloud infrastructure to SMB clients under recurring contract.

### Why UK IT MSPs Over Alternatives

| Dimension | UK Tech Recruitment (prior niche) | UK IT MSPs (confirmed niche) |
|---|---|---|
| Total addressable firms | ~31,000 (but ICP-fit ~1,400) | **15,000+ MSPs** in UK |
| Apollo validated contacts | ~500–800 after dedup | **~4,682 at correct filter settings** |
| Decision-maker reachability | MD/Founder, often gatekept | **MD/Founder directly reachable — owner-operated** |
| Founder's domain expertise | External category | **Operated in MSP industry since 2017 — peer positioning** |
| Contract value per win | £7,500–£32,000 per placement | **£10,000–£42,000/year ARR per SMB client (recurring)** |
| Revenue stability for buyer | Lumpy per-placement | **Recurring MRR — contract-for-life economics** |
| Structural pain | Mandate scarcity | **Referrals unpredictable, no systematic BD, owner is sole new-business engine** |

### Market Context (UK 2025–2026)

- **15,000+ MSP firms in the UK** — large, fragmented, under-consolidated addressable market
- **Average managed service contract:** £800–£3,500/month per SMB client
- **Annual contract value per SMB client:** £10,000–£42,000 (recurring)
- **Pain concentrated at 5–50 person independent MSPs:** too big to survive on referrals alone, too small to hire a dedicated BD/sales person
- **Primary pain:** new client acquisition — not service delivery. Engineers are capable; the pipeline of new SMBs to talk to is the bottleneck
- **Most have tried Google Ads, SEO, and generic marketing agencies** — and watched all of it fail, because MSP BD is a relationship/trust sale, not a lead-form sale
- **The owner/MD is the sole BD engine.** When referrals slow, the pipeline slows. There is no systematic outbound infrastructure behind the business

### The Exact Target

| **Company age** | 3–12 years old |
| **Annual revenue** | £500k–£5m |
| **Business model** | Managed service contracts (MRR-based, recurring) |
| **Tech niche** | Microsoft partner, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, IT support / helpdesk |
| **Geography** | London → Manchester → Leeds → Bristol → Birmingham → Edinburgh |
| **Decision-maker title** | Managing Director, Founder, CEO, Owner |
| **BD situation** | Owner is sole BD engine; tried Google Ads / SEO / marketing agency; no systematic outreach to SMBs |

### Why 5–50 (Sweetspot 8–25)

- **Floor at 5:** Below 5 people, the MSP is usually a one-man-band with the owner still on the tools. Pain exists but the budget signal is weak and the business can't absorb new managed service contracts at pace.
- **Sweetspot 8–25:** Engineers are in place, delivery is stable, the MD is no longer on tickets full-time — and yet the new-business function is still just the MD on referrals. This is the acute window.
- **Ceiling at 50:** Above 50 employees, MSPs tend to have hired a dedicated sales or BD person, or are being courted by PE consolidators. The owner-as-sole-BD dynamic begins to break down.
- **Hard ceiling — exclude:** Any firm with a visible dedicated Sales Director on LinkedIn, or any PE-backed / publicly listed IT firm.

### The "About to Buy" Signal Cluster (Tier 1)

A contact hitting 4+ of these is a Tier 1 dial:
1. **Open job posting** for engineer, account manager, or service desk staff (growth without confirmed contract pipeline)
2. **Headcount 8–25** (Goldilocks zone — delivery capacity in place, no BD hire yet)
3. **Company age 3–10 years old** (past survival, before consolidation)
4. **No dedicated sales director** visible on LinkedIn (confirms owner is sole BD engine)
5. **Uses ConnectWise / Autotask / Datto / Kaseya** (confirmed professional MSP, not IT generalist)
6. **MD / Founder actively posting on LinkedIn** about growth, hiring, or new services (active, reachable, self-selecting into visibility)

### Fee Economics (Why They Can Afford This)

| Contract Type | Annual ARR |
|---|---|
| 10-seat SMB (break-fix → managed) | £9,600–£18,000 |
| 30-seat SMB (mid-size business) | £18,000–£30,000 |
| 50-seat SMB (growing business) | £24,000–£42,000 |
| 100-seat SMB (larger enterprise) | £36,000–£72,000 |

**One new 30-seat managed service contract covers the £3,500 build fee in the first 2–3 months.** Every contract after that is compounding return on infrastructure the MSP owns permanently. The ROI conversation is immediate and inarguable.

## 6. THE GUARANTEE MECHANICS

### What Counts as "Qualified"

A qualified prospect conversation requires **ALL** of the following:

1. A two-way exchange (not a rejection or remove request)
2. The prospect has confirmed a current or near-future IT need, **or** is open to reviewing their current IT arrangement
3. The exchange contains enough information to determine whether a managed service contract is possible (business size, current IT situation — break-fix / unhappy with existing MSP / growing — rough timeline)
4. The prospect has agreed to continue (call booked, **or** confirmed they'll respond to a brief)

### What Doesn't Count

- *"We're fine with our current IT"* — unless they add *"but we're growing"* (that's a pipeline conversation)
- *"We handle IT internally"* with no further engagement
- Spam complaints / remove requests

### If Guarantee Triggers (< 5 conversations by Day 30)

1. **Day 30 review call:** count together using HubSpot pipeline data
2. **Trigger confirmed:** IGC continues running the system — sequences active — at **no charge**
3. **Extension:** 30 additional days. IGC actively optimises (not just monitors): A/B test underperforming message, try second audience segment (swap FD/Ops Director in), adjust send times, re-engage "nearly qualified" contacts
4. **After extension:** if 5 cumulative conversations achieved, engagement closes and retainer pitch proceeds

**Client obligations (in written agreement):**
- Respond to "Replied" notifications within 1 business day
- Log in to LinkedIn minimum 3x/week (keeps automation session alive)
- Not pause or alter sequences without IGC approval
- Attend kickoff and pre-launch calls

Client inaction causing guarantee failure is not IGC's liability.

### The Number 5 — Validation

At conservative LinkedIn benchmarks:
- 400 connection requests sent
- 30% acceptance = 120 connected
- 10% reply rate = 12 replies
- 50% qualify = **6 qualified conversations**

Five is achievable at conservative rates and meaningful to the MSP owner (5 SMB conversations = a real new-contract pipeline). The number is correct for UK MSP client acquisition.

## 8. COLD CALL SCRIPT

### Pre-Call 3-Second Mental Model

> *"This person is running a [X]-person IT MSP. They founded it in [year]. They are the sole BD engine. Right now, their [engineers are busy on delivery / they've been posting on LinkedIn without generating new contracts / they're growing headcount without confirmed new contract pipeline]. Today is the right call because [specific signal]."*

This mental model runs before the dial connects. It grounds the opener in something true about the prospect before any script word is spoken.

### The Core Opening — Version 1 (Tier 1 Job Posting Signal)

Use when the MSP is hiring an engineer or account manager.

> *"Hi [Name], my name is [Your name], calling from IGC. I saw [Company] is hiring a [engineer / account manager] — and I'll be honest, that's exactly the situation where I call. Most MSP owners I speak to at that point are thinking about where the new managed service contracts are coming from to support that growth. Is that on your radar at all?"*

**Why it works:**
- Opens with a specific, verifiable observation (the job posting) — not a generic claim
- Names the exact pain (new contract pipeline to absorb the new hire) before they have to admit it
- Ends with a yes/no question they'll answer honestly
- Doesn't mention a product in the first 15 seconds

### The Core Opening — Version 2 (No Job Posting / Base Signal)

Use when no active hiring signal but the firm fits ICP.

> *"Hi [Name], my name is [Your name] from IGC. We work exclusively with independent IT MSPs in the UK — typically 5 to 50 people — building the outreach infrastructure that generates new managed service contract conversations, so the BD isn't entirely owner-dependent. I've been in the MSP space myself since 2017. Do you have 90 seconds?"*

**Why the 2017 line matters:** Every MSP owner on the other end of this call has taken a thousand cold calls from people who obviously don't understand the industry. "In the MSP space myself since 2017" earns 60 seconds of real attention. This is peer-to-peer, not vendor-to-prospect.

### Discovery Questions (Once You Have Permission)

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | *"How are you winning new managed service contracts at the moment — is it mainly referrals, or do you have any systematic outreach running?"* | Confirms the pain pattern (referral-dependent) |
| Q2 | *"How's the new business pipeline looking — are you winning contracts consistently, or is that the bit you're actively trying to fix?"* | Urgency check |
| Q3 | *"Have you tried anything on the BD side — LinkedIn outreach, Google Ads, a marketing agency, anything like that?"* | Past-attempt inventory (→ differentiation angle) |
| Q4 | *"When you think about new business right now — biggest bottleneck: finding the right SMBs to talk to, doing the outreach consistently, or converting conversations to contracts?"* | Isolates the bottleneck they'll buy to fix |
| Q5 | *"Have you looked at bringing anyone in to help — a BD hire, an agency, a tool?"* | Budget + build-vs-buy signal |
| Q6 | *"When it comes to investing in BD infrastructure — is that purely your call, or is there a business partner involved?"* | Decision-maker confirmation |
| Q7 | *"What's driving the timing on this — is there a pressure point making new business more urgent right now?"* | Trigger event / compelling reason to act |

### Qualification Criteria (Before Booking Discovery Call)

- **Size:** 5–50 employees confirmed
- **Pain confirmed:** acknowledges new contract pipeline is a gap or vulnerability
- **Decision-maker:** MD / Founder / Owner is the person on the call (or will be on the discovery call)
- **No hard blocker:** no active long-term agency contract already delivering results, no business wind-down / sale in progress

> *"We build a client acquisition system in 30 days — LinkedIn outreach sequences targeting MDs and Directors at SMBs in your patch who are on break-fix, unhappy with their current MSP, or growing fast enough to need proper IT infrastructure. **"I'm too busy to run outreach."**
> *"You don't run it. We build it, it runs on your LinkedIn, and you take the calls when prospects respond. Your job is to respond to replies within a business day — that's it. The outreach happens automatically."*

**"What makes you different?"**
> *"Two things: we build it and hand it over — you own it permanently, no dependency on us. And we guarantee 5 qualified conversations in 30 days or we don't charge for the extension. Every other option either runs it for you forever or teaches you to run it yourself. We do neither — we build the infrastructure and leave."*

**"How much does it cost?"**
> *"£3,500 to build the system in 30 days. After that, £1,750 a month if you want us to operate it — entirely optional. One new managed service contract covers the build fee. If you sign one 30-seat business in month one, you've broken even."*
